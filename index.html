Features

Built for loot-driven raids

Everything you need to read a map, price a route, and decide when to extract — in one lightweight overlay.

📦

Item ESP

Highlights loose loot through walls and terrain with clear labels, so a GPU or LEDX never slips past your route again.

🗄️

Container Overlay

Draws crates, safes, weapon boxes, and jackets with their type, letting you prioritize the containers worth the detour.

📏

Distance Readouts

Live distance in meters for every tracked spawn, so you know if a high-value item is one room away or across the map.

💎

High-Value Filter

Toggle value tiers to hide junk and surface only barter, quest, and flea-market items worth carrying out.

🗺️

All Maps Supported

Customs, Woods, Interchange, Reserve, Lighthouse, Streets, and Labs — with spawn data tuned per map layout.

⚡

Low Overhead

Lightweight render path and lean memory footprint keep frame pacing stable even during heavy firefights.
